Ask Us Your Questions
Do you offer free quotes?
Yes. Quotes are free and no-pressure. Tell us about the job through the contact form or give us a call at 250-961-5715 and we’ll get back to you with a straight answer and an upfront price.
What areas do you serve?
We’re based in Prince George, BC and also serve Mackenzie, Vanderhoof, Fort St. James, Quesnel and the communities in between. Don’t see your town? Call us — chances are we cover it.
How quickly will I hear back?
We usually reply within one business day. For anything urgent, give us a call — you’ll talk to Landon directly, not a call centre.
Should I repair or replace my boiler, furnace or hot water tank?
It depends on the age, condition and efficiency of the equipment. We’ll give you an honest comparison of repair vs. replacement and never upsell you on capacity you don’t need.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Accelerate Plumbing & Heating is a licensed and insured, Red Seal trade, and we do our gas fitting safely and to code.
